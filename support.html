1Hours, support and updates

Brick House is run to be available at all hours of every day. Its support hours, in which a director is on duty, are Monday to Friday, 09:00 to 17:00 UK time, except bank holidays in England.

Outside support hours Brick House keeps running and its monitoring alerts still reach both directors, each at his own address, but no one is on duty: an incident is taken up when support hours next begin, or sooner where a director sees it and can act. Each Trust keeps its own way of working without Brick House, as its own business continuity plan sets.

Brick House is provided as a service, and every Trust uses its one current release, which the company keeps up to date with nothing for a Trust or its users to install or apply. A fix for a high or critical vulnerability is applied within 14 days of its release, and each Trust using Brick House is then told what was fixed. Brick House is supported throughout each Trust's contract for it, and the company tells each Trust at least a year before it stops supporting Brick House.

3Reporting a fault or an incident

A user reports a fault to their Trust's IT service desk or to the company at support@bluespaceportal.com, the address Brick House itself gives for support. A Trust's IT service desk, or its named contacts for Brick House, report to the same address and, for an incident that cannot wait, by telephone to the director on duty, whose number each Trust's named contacts are given when the Trust starts to use Brick House.

A report gives as much as is known of: who reports it and how to reach them; the Trust; when it began; what is affected, Brick House as a whole or a part of it; how many users; whether patient care is affected or patient data is at risk; and what has been tried.

8Escalation and complaints

Anyone not satisfied with how the company handles a fault, an incident or a request, a Trust, a user, NHS England or another supplier connected to the same NHS England services, may escalate it to the director not handling it, or complain, at the support address or to either director. The company acknowledges a complaint within two working days, answers it within 20 working days, and says what it has changed. A complaint about personal data follows PL-03.
