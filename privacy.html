  • Our two directors.

  • Our bank, which holds payment details as a controller in its own right.
  • Our accountant, which is given the records it needs to prepare our accounts, returns and filings and holds them as a controller in its own right, and which, being at our registered office, receives the post sent to us there and passes it to us.

4Where it is kept, and how it is kept secure

It is kept in our email; in our working files, which hold copies of some correspondence and, once a year, an export of all our email, and are backed up in the United Kingdom; and, for payment details, with our bank, and, for what our accounts, returns and filings need, with our accountant. What we show our AI assistant is held by its provider in the United States, under the safeguards its own privacy policy sets for transfers. Our email is held by a provider that may store it in any country where it or its sub-processors have facilities; a transfer to a country the UK has not found to protect personal data adequately is covered by standard contractual clauses with their UK supplementary terms, a copy of which you can ask us for.

We keep it secure. Within the company only our two directors can reach it, each as far as his role needs; our email and our bank account are behind two-step verification, and every device we use is encrypted. If a breach of your data is likely to put you at high risk, we tell you without undue delay.

6Your rights

You can ask us to give you a copy of your data, to correct it, to delete it, to restrict how we use it, to send it to another organisation where the law gives that right, and to stop using it where we rely on our legitimate interests. If you ask us to stop sending you marketing, we always stop. Ask in any way you like, to either director or at security@bluespaceportal.com. We check that the request is yours, and answer within one month, or within two further months for a complex request, in which case we tell you why within the first month. We do not charge, unless a request is plainly unfounded or excessive. Some rights are limited by law: we cannot delete records the law requires us to keep.

7Complaints

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your data, please tell us first, in any way you like, for instance at security@bluespaceportal.com or by post. We acknowledge your complaint within 30 days, look into it, keep you told of our progress, and tell you the outcome without undue delay. You can also complain to the ICO, at ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint or on 0303 123 1113.

8Brick House and patient data

Brick House is our record system for NHS neonatal transport services and neonatal units. Each NHS Trust that uses it is the controller of the records it holds there, about babies, their parents and the Trust's own staff, and we handle them only on that Trust's instructions, as its processor. The Trust's privacy notice tells you how it uses your data, and a request or complaint about that data is best made to the Trust: if you send one to us, we pass it to the Trust within two working days and help it to answer.

We comply with the national data opt-out. If you do not want confidential information about your health and care used for purposes beyond your own care, such as research and planning, you can set or change your choice at nhs.uk/your-nhs-data-matters.

If you are a member of NHS staff and sign in to Brick House with your NHS Care Identity, NHS England checks who you are and tells Brick House the result: your identifier in that service and, where Brick House uses them, your name, organisation and role, with the address of the device you sign in from. We handle that result on NHS England's behalf, as its processor, and NHS England's own privacy notice, on its website, says how it uses your Care Identity. Everything else Brick House holds about you, your account, your role and the record of what you do there, we handle for your Trust, as this section says above.

GitHub, which hosts them, logs the IP address of each visitor for security. That log is GitHub's own record, kept under GitHub's privacy statement, and may be held in the United States.
