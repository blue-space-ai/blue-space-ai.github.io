This notice is for anyone whose personal data Blue Space AI Limited holds for its own purposes: the people we deal with at NHS bodies, regulators, suppliers and prospective customers, the people we pay or who pay us, and our directors. It says what we hold and why, who sees it, how long we keep it, and what you can do about it. If your details are in Brick House, the record system we provide to neonatal services, section 8 explains why your hospital's privacy notice applies to them rather than this one.

3. Who sees it

  • Our two directors.
  • Our service providers, which handle it for us under written contracts meeting UK GDPR Article 28: Google, which runs our email, and Microsoft, which holds our controlled documents.
  • Our bank, Revolut, which holds payment details as a controller in its own right.
  • Public bodies, where the law requires it: Companies House, HM Revenue and Customs, the ICO, and NHS England for its Data Security and Protection Toolkit.

We share it with no one else unless the law requires it or you ask us to.

4. Where it is kept

Our controlled documents are held by Microsoft in the United Kingdom. Our email is held by Google, which may store it in any country where it or its sub-processors have facilities; a transfer to a country the UK has not found to protect personal data adequately is covered by standard contractual clauses with their UK supplementary terms, in Google's data processing terms.

5. How long we keep it

  • Correspondence and contact details: six years from our last dealings, unless they belong to a record below.
  • Payments, invoices and tax records: six years from the end of the financial year they relate to.
  • Directors' decisions and the company's statutory records: at least ten years.
  • A request about your rights, and our answer: three years from the answer, or six where it is appealed.
  • A complaint: ten years.

Then we delete it securely.

6. Your rights

You can ask us to give you a copy of your data, to correct it, to delete it, to restrict how we use it, to send it to another organisation where the law gives that right, and to stop using it where we rely on our legitimate interests. Ask in any way you like, to either director or at security@bluespaceportal.com. We check that the request is yours, and answer within one month, or within two further months for a complex request, in which case we tell you why within the first month. We do not charge, unless a request is plainly unfounded or excessive. Some rights are limited by law: we cannot delete records the law requires us to keep.

7. Complaints

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your data, please tell us first, at security@bluespaceportal.com or by post. We acknowledge your complaint within 30 days, look into it, keep you told of our progress, and tell you the outcome without undue delay. You can also complain to the ICO, at ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint or on 0303 123 1113.

8. Brick House and patient data

Brick House is a record system we provide to NHS neonatal transport services and neonatal units. Each NHS Trust that uses it is the controller of the records it holds there, about babies, their parents and the Trust's own staff, and we handle them only on that Trust's instructions, as its processor. The Trust's privacy notice tells you how it uses your data, and a request or complaint about that data is best made to the Trust: if you send one to us, we pass it to the Trust within two working days and help it to answer. At present Brick House holds test records only, made up for the purpose, and we hold no patient's data.

We comply with the national data opt-out. If you do not want confidential information about your health and care used for purposes beyond your own care, such as research and planning, you can set or change your choice at nhs.uk/your-nhs-data-matters.

9. Our website

Our website, blue-space-ai.com, sets no cookies, uses no analytics and asks you for nothing. GitHub, which hosts it, logs the IP address of each visitor and stores it for security, as GitHub's own record under its privacy statement, which may be held in the United States.

10. Changes to this notice

We review this notice at least once a year, and whenever our use of personal data changes. The version and date at the top are those of the current notice.
