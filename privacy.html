If your details are in Brick House, the record system we provide to neonatal services, section 8 explains why your hospital's privacy notice applies to them rather than this one.

3. Who sees it

  • Our two directors.
  • Our service providers, which handle it for us under written contracts meeting UK GDPR Article 28: Google, which runs our email, and Microsoft, which holds our controlled documents.
  • Our bank, Revolut, which holds payment details as a controller in its own right.
  • GitHub, which hosts our website, Squarespace, which registers our domains, and Apple, which keeps one director's passwords end-to-end encrypted, each holding a director's account details as a controller in its own right.
  • Public bodies: Companies House, HM Revenue and Customs and the ICO, where the law requires it; and, on our legitimate interest in meeting what these bodies ask of a supplier (f), NHS England, which holds our directors' names and roles in its Data Security and Protection Toolkit and a director's details in our account with its National Service Desk, and the Cabinet Office, which holds a director's details in our supplier record on its Central Digital Platform.

We share it with no one else unless the law requires it or you ask us to.

4. Where it is kept

Our controlled documents are held by Microsoft in the United Kingdom, though Microsoft keeps a few kinds of account data for that service in the United States, as GitHub, which hosts our website, and Squarespace, our domain registrar, keep a director's account details there; each is certified to the UK Extension to the EU-US Data Privacy Framework, which the UK treats as giving adequate protection. Our email is held by Google, which may store it in any country where it or its sub-processors have facilities; a transfer to a country the UK has not found to protect personal data adequately is covered by standard contractual clauses with their UK supplementary terms, in Google's data processing terms.

5. How long we keep it

  • Correspondence, contact details and our directors' details in our accounts with public bodies: six years from our last dealings, unless they belong to another record in this list, whose period they then follow.
  • Payments, invoices and tax records: six years from the end of the financial year they relate to.
  • Contracts and tenders: six years after the contract ends.
  • Directors' decisions and the company's statutory records: at least ten years.
  • Our documents, which name our directors in their roles: the life of the company and six years, or at least ten years after the last medical device they cover is placed on the market, with every version kept.
  • A request about your rights, and our answer: three years from the answer, or six where it is appealed.
  • A complaint: ten years.
  • An incident or breach: ten years, or twenty for a serious one.
  • Our list of people who have asked us not to send them marketing: for as long as we send marketing.
  • Our records of our directors' devices: six years; of their training, agreements and access: six years after they leave. They may then stand in our repository's history, which keeps every version.

Then we review it, and delete it securely unless a reason to keep it remains, recording our decision.

6. Your rights

You can ask us to give you a copy of your data, to correct it, to delete it, to restrict how we use it, to send it to another organisation where the law gives that right, and to stop using it where we rely on our legitimate interests, which for marketing we always do. Ask in any way you like, to either director or at security@bluespaceportal.com. We check that the request is yours, and answer within one month, or within two further months for a complex request, in which case we tell you why within the first month. We do not charge, unless a request is plainly unfounded or excessive. Some rights are limited by law: we cannot delete records the law requires us to keep.

7. Complaints

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your data, please tell us first, at security@bluespaceportal.com or by post. We acknowledge your complaint within 30 days, look into it, keep you told of our progress, and tell you the outcome without undue delay. You can also complain to the ICO, at ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint or on 0303 123 1113.

8. Brick House and patient data

Brick House is a record system we provide to NHS neonatal transport services and neonatal units. Each NHS Trust that uses it is the controller of the records it holds there, about babies, their parents and the Trust's own staff, and we handle them only on that Trust's instructions, as its processor. The Trust's privacy notice tells you how it uses your data, and a request or complaint about that data is best made to the Trust: if you send one to us, we pass it to the Trust within two working days and help it to answer. At present Brick House holds test records only, made up for the purpose, and we hold no patient's data.

We comply with the national data opt-out. If you do not want confidential information about your health and care used for purposes beyond your own care, such as research and planning, you can set or change your choice at nhs.uk/your-nhs-data-matters.

9. Our website

Our website, blue-space-ai.com, sets no cookies, uses no analytics and asks you for nothing. GitHub, which hosts it, logs the IP address of each visitor and stores it for security, as GitHub's own record under its privacy statement, which may be held in the United States.
